# 🎯 Project Overview

Banks generate large volumes of customer data, but raw data alone does not provide meaningful business insights.

This project transforms a customer dataset into an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that enables stakeholders to quickly understand:

- Who the customers are
- How customers are segmented
- Which customers are actively engaged
- How customer churn is distributed
- How financial indicators vary across customers
- The relationship between credit score, balance, and monthly income
- How customers are distributed across loyalty levels

The result is a centralized **UK Bank Customer Analytics Dashboard** designed to support data-driven decision-making.

# 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights four key customer metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 👥 **Total Customers** | Overall customer population |
| 💰 **Average Balance** | Average financial balance across customers |
| 💵 **Average Monthly Income** | Average monthly income |
| 📊 **Average Credit Score** | Overall customer credit profile |

These KPIs provide a high-level snapshot before exploring individual customer behaviour.

# 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

## 👥 Customer Demographics

The dashboard analyses customer distribution by gender to provide a quick overview of the customer base.

---

## 🔄 Customer Churn Analysis

Customers are classified into:

- 🟢 Active
- 🔴 Churned

This allows stakeholders to monitor customer retention and identify potential areas for further churn investigation.

---

## 📈 Customer Engagement Analysis

Customers are grouped into engagement categories and compared across churn status.

This helps identify whether lower engagement may be associated with increased customer churn.

---

## 💳 Credit & Financial Behaviour

The dashboard explores relationships between:

- Credit Score
- Customer Balance
- Monthly Income

The scatter and combination charts help identify financial patterns across the customer base.

---

## 🏆 Loyalty Analysis

Customers are grouped into:

- 🥉 Bronze
- 🥈 Silver
- 🥇 Gold

This provides insight into the distribution of customers across loyalty tiers.

---

## 🎯 Customer Segmentation

Customers are categorised into:

- Emerging
- Mass
- Priority
- Affluent

This segmentation provides a clearer view of the bank's customer portfolio and supports targeted business strategies.
